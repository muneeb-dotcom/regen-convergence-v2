# Day 18 — Literature tiers

## Nfatc1 — Known regulator
Calcineurin-NFAT signaling directly implicated wound healing regulation
(fibrosis, keloid, ulcer processes). NFATc1 elevated in hair follicle
stem cells during wound-adjacent epidermis regeneration. Direct role.

## Sfrp4 — Known regulator
Strong direct evidence. Wnt inhibitor SFRP4 controls fibrotic vs
regenerative wound healing fate in mouse skin (WIHN model). Macrophage
phagocytosis of SFRP4 drives fibrosis; SFRP4 loss favors regeneration.
Directly matches this project's regeneration vs scarring framework.

## Saa3 — Studied elsewhere
Extensive literature, inflammation/immunity context (colitis, obesity,
influenza, adipose tissue). No direct regeneration/wound healing role
found. Acute-phase, innate immune signaling gene.

## Dusp4 — Studied elsewhere
MAPK/ERK phosphatase, negative regulator MAPK signaling. Literature in
cancer, cardiomyopathy, macrophage differentiation. No direct
regeneration-specific study found.
